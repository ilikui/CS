# 写作｜功能表达与自编范文

> 模板是结构提示，不是整篇背诵材料。考试时必须根据题目对象、目的和信息点改写。

## 应用文结构模板
**Dear [Name],**

I am writing to [state the purpose]. [Give the first required detail]. In addition, [give the second detail and any necessary explanation]. I believe [brief reason or benefit].

Please let me know if you need any further information. I look forward to hearing from you.

**Yours sincerely,**
[Name]

## 自编范文：邀请参加学习交流活动
Dear Alex,

I am writing to invite you to join a study-sharing event on effective ways of learning with digital tools. It will take place in Room 204 at 3 p.m. next Friday.

During the event, several students will share how they organize online materials, take notes and review what they have learned. I believe your experience with digital learning would be valuable to everyone, and I hope you can share your ideas with us.

Please let me know whether you are available. I look forward to seeing you.

Yours sincerely,
Li

## 范文拆解
- 第一段完成邀请目的、主题、时间和地点。
- 第二段说明活动内容，并解释邀请对方的原因。
- 第三段提出确认请求并礼貌收尾。
- 语言以准确、清晰为主，没有为了复杂而堆砌从句。

## 图表作文句型
- The chart compares ... over the period from ... to ...
- The proportion of ... rose from ... to ...
- By comparison, the figure for ... remained relatively stable.
- One possible explanation is that ...
- These figures suggest a change in ..., although further evidence is needed to identify its causes.
