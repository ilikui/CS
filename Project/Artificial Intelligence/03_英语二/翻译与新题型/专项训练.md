# 翻译与新题型｜专项训练卡

## 翻译训练
**原句（自编）：** The growing availability of online resources has changed not only how people learn but also what they expect from educational institutions.

**拆解：**
- 主语：The growing availability of online resources
- 谓语：has changed
- 宾语并列结构：not only how people learn but also what they expect from educational institutions
- 译文：在线资源日益丰富，不仅改变了人们的学习方式，也改变了他们对教育机构的期待。

**自查：** availability 不是“能力”；not only...but also... 两部分应保持并列；how/what 引导的从句整体作宾语。

## 语篇衔接练习
连接词功能：
- 转折：however, nevertheless, in contrast
- 因果：therefore, consequently, as a result
- 递进：furthermore, moreover, in addition
- 举例：for instance, for example
- 总结：in short, overall, to sum up

## 排序练习
A. This is why feedback should be specific and timely.
B. Students need to know which part of their work requires improvement.
C. General praise alone rarely tells learners what to do next.

**参考顺序：C → B → A。** C 提出泛泛表扬的不足；B 解释学习者需要具体信息；A 用 This 回指前述观点并总结建议。

## 复盘模板
记录段落主题、衔接词、代词指向、句间关系、候选顺序及最终验证过程。不要只记正确顺序，要记为什么其他顺序不成立。
